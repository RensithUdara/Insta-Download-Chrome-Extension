# 📥 Instagram Downloader - Chrome Extension

Download photos, videos, reels, stories, and highlights from Instagram with one click!

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Install** the extension from Chrome Web Store
2. **Visit** any Instagram page
3. **Click** the extension icon to see available media
4. **Download** instantly with one click

## ✨ Features

### Media Downloads
- 📸 **Photos** - Download single or gallery photos
- 🎬 **Reels & Videos** - Get your favorite reels and video posts
- 📖 **Stories & Highlights** - Save stories before they expire
- 📺 **Instagram TV** - Download IGTV content

### Smart Tools
- 📦 **Batch Download** - Download multiple items as ZIP
- 🎯 **Selection Mode** - Choose specific media to download
- ⚙️ **Quality Control** - High, medium, or low resolution options
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** - Easy on the eyes

### Privacy First
- 🔒 **100% Local Processing** - All operations happen in your browser
- 🔐 **No Tracking** - No cookies, analytics, or data collection
- 🔑 **No Account Access** - Never asks for your Instagram password
- 📡 **No External Servers** - Data never leaves your device

## 🎯 Use Cases

- ✅ Back up your own photos and videos
- ✅ Save expiring stories before they disappear
- ✅ Download reels for offline viewing
- ✅ Collect favorite posts from inspirational accounts

## 📋 Permissions Explained

We only request permissions we absolutely need:

- **Active Tab** - Know when you're on Instagram
- **Scripting** - Scan pages for downloadable media
- **Downloads** - Save files to your computer
- **Storage** - Remember your preferences
- **Tabs** - Identify Instagram tabs

**None of these permissions are used for tracking.**

## 🔧 Settings

### Quality Preference
Choose your preferred download quality:
- 🔥 **High** - Best quality, larger file size
- 👍 **Medium** - Balanced quality and size
- 📱 **Low** - Smaller file size, lower quality

### Auto ZIP
Enable automatic ZIP creation for batch downloads

### Dark Mode
Toggle dark theme for comfortable viewing

## 📥 Download Locations

Downloads are saved to your default Downloads folder:
- 🪟 Windows: `C:\Users\YourName\Downloads\`
- 🍎 macOS: `~/Downloads/`
- 🐧 Linux: `~/Downloads/`

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Media not detecting?
1. Refresh the Instagram page
2. Wait for page to fully load
3. Check if the content is actually downloadable (some stories may have restrictions)

### Download failed?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure sufficient disk space
3. Try a different quality setting
4. Refresh and try again

### Extension not working?
1. Refresh Instagram page
2. Click the extension icon in toolbar
3. Check if extension is enabled in chrome://extensions/
4. Try disabling and re-enabling the extension
